YOUR PRACTICE, IN YOUR POCKET

Everything inside

All of TAZAWWUD,
in one app.

Each area below is part of the app today.

QUR’AN

Reading and listening

Mushaf page view and verse view. Contents lists every surah. Go to a surah and ayah directly. Translations, recitation audio and bookmarks.

HADITH

Hadith collections

Read the Hadith collections in the app, with the grading shown where the source provides it. Download collections for offline reading.

SALAH

Prayer times and Qiblah

Prayer times for your location or a city you choose, with the calculation method and Asr school you prefer. A Qiblah compass shows the direction of the Ka’bah.

ADHAN

Alerts and reminders

Prayer alerts with the Adhan or a simple chime, and a choice of reciters. Optional Qur’an, Adhkar and Friday reminders. Turn each one on or off.

DAILY

Daily tools

Dua and Dhikr, the 99 Names of Allah, a Tasbih counter, the Hijri calendar, and Stories.

NEARBY

Mosque finder

Find mosques near you, or search a place. Results open in Google Maps.

Android app: prayer alerts and the next-prayer countdown can run in the notification shade while the app is closed. The Android app is not yet on Google Play.
