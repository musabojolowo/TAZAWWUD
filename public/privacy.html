Your information, clearly explained

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This policy describes how the TAZAWWUD web app handles information when you use it. TAZAWWUD provides Qur’an reading, prayer tools, daily guidance, bookmarks, and related features.

TAZAWWUD does not require an account for ordinary browsing. Some features can use Firebase Authentication and Realtime Database when you sign in or save information.

Information you choose to provide

  • Account information: If you choose Google sign-in, Firebase Authentication processes account identifiers such as your UID, name, and email as made available by Google. The app can also create an anonymous Firebase account to support account-scoped features.
  • Bookmarks and preferences: Bookmarks, reading progress, and selected preferences may be stored in this browser and, when signed in, in your private Firebase Realtime Database account path.
  • Location: If you request location-based prayer times or Qiblah direction, your browser asks permission. Location may be used in the browser to calculate direction or request prayer-time data. You can instead choose a city. Do not grant browser location permission if you do not want to share location with this feature.
  • Reminders: Reminder labels, times, and enabled state are stored locally in your browser. If you enable notifications, the browser handles notification permission and display.
  • Admin content: Stories and daily featured content are stored in Firebase and are publicly readable in the app. Only accounts authorized by the project administrator can write that content.

Information handled by your browser

TAZAWWUD uses browser storage for preferences, bookmarks, reading progress, and reminder settings. Its service worker can cache app files and content you have opened to support offline use. Clearing site data in your browser removes locally stored information and cached files.

Service providers and external sources

The app uses Firebase Authentication and Realtime Database for account and sync features. Qur’an text and translations, prayer times, Hadith and commentary data, and recitation audio may be requested from external providers, including AlQuran Cloud, Aladhan, MP3Quran, and public repositories/CDNs. Google Fonts may receive standard connection information from your browser when fonts load. These providers process requests under their own privacy terms and policies.

How information is used

Information is used to provide the features you request: signing in, syncing your own data, remembering preferences, calculating prayer-related information, delivering reminders, and displaying content. TAZAWWUD does not use your bookmarks or preferences to build advertising profiles.

Sharing and public content

Your private bookmarks and account preferences are protected by the app’s Firebase database rules and are scoped to the signed-in UID. Stories and daily featured entries are intentionally public app content. No service can be guaranteed secure in every circumstance; protect access to your account and device.

Your choices

  • Use the app without signing in where the feature allows it.
  • Decline or revoke location and notification permissions in your browser/device settings.
  • Use the app’s Delete My Data control for account data managed by the app, or clear browser site data to remove local information.

Review form

The review form on the landing page is provided by Google Forms. If you answer it, Google stores your answers, and your name if you choose to give one. The form is used only to review and improve TAZAWWUD before it is launched. The answers are not used to identify you in the app, and the app does not read them. Google’s own privacy policy also applies to the form.

Children

TAZAWWUD is a general audience informational app and is not designed to collect personal information from children. A parent or guardian should supervise a child’s use of sign-in and browser permissions.

Policy changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the app changes. The revised date will be shown at the top of this page. For privacy questions, use the official TAZAWWUD project contact channel through which you obtained the app.
